How to read the floor

  1. Boxes are tasks. Find the machine with a full meter and a growing pile in front of it. That is the constraint.
  2. The optimizations for each machine are listed in the column under it. Hover one to read it, click to install. Amber edge: you can afford it. Dashed: it says what it needs first. Green: installed. Ten days later the log says whether shipped tasks per day moved, and the sparklines show it.
  3. Early spend starts at $0 and adds up the price of every capacity optimization you install at a station that is not the constraint at that moment. You may need it later, but today it buys nothing. The lower it is at the end, the better your grade.
  4. When one pile drains, another starts. Go back to step 1.
  5. Every signal has a traffic light: green is healthy, amber is worth watching, red is a problem. Hover a signal to read what it means and what usually fixes it.
  6. Red boxes on the top pipe are rework going back to Code. A red beacon means an incident has stopped a station. Wait it out, or pay to clear it from that station's column.
  7. Each person on the floor is a human gate. Automate the gate and they go home. You win when all five lamps are out.
