## The scene

An agent starts on a two-line ticket. Forty minutes in, it stops: should deleted users keep their invoices? You were in a meeting. It waited three hours for a one-word answer.

## The play

Before work starts, the agent interrogates the ticket and the code and hands back a decision brief: the spec, the screens, and every open question as multiple choice with its recommended answer already ticked. You fix what is wrong and paste it back. Two minutes, once.

## Smells

- Agents stop mid-task to ask what was meant.
- Rework that traces back to a vague ticket.
- People answering agent questions all day in small pieces.

## First move

On your next task, ask the agent to list every decision it would otherwise make silently, with its recommendation. Count how many you would have answered differently.

## In Lights Out

Decision briefs raise Spec capacity and cut rework by ten points.
