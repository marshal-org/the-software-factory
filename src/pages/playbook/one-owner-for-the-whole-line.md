## The scene

The developers get agents and their output multiplies. Nobody told QA. A few weeks later QA is drowning in work that arrives faster than they can test it. You help QA, and now the CI servers that DevOps has run for years cannot keep up. Each team was measured on its own station, so on paper every step was a success.

## The play

Someone owns the line end to end, with the authority to spend at whichever station is holding it today, in whichever team that station sits. Before you start, walk the line with every team lead and tell them the order in which you expect to reach them.

## Smells

- The AI initiative belongs to one team.
- Every team reports its own speed-up and nobody reports what ships.
- The team downstream finds out by drowning.

## First move

Name the owner. Then go to the team that sits after the one you are about to speed up and tell them: you are next.

## In Lights Out

In Lights Out one player owns all seven stations and can spend anywhere. That is the part most organisations are missing.
