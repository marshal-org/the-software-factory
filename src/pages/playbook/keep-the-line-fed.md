## The scene

Friday afternoon. The pile of well-defined tickets is empty and the agents are idle. An engineer starts a refactoring nobody asked for, to keep them busy.

## The play

Specifying becomes the main job. Engineers move from writing code to deciding outcomes. Agents turn support tickets, error reports and telemetry into drafted specs, and people accept, reject and rank them.

## Smells

- Agents idle because the backlog is thin or vague.
- Engineers inventing work to keep sessions busy.
- Product decisions being made inside pull requests.

## First move

Count the tickets that are ready to start today and divide by what your line ships per day. Under a week means this is your next constraint.

With every lamp out, Spec is the last constraint. There are five ways to raise it.
