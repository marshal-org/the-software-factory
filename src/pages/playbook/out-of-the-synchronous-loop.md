## The scene

Eight sessions in eight tabs. Approve here, read a spec there, answer a question, click through a feature. It feels like flying. Then you go to sleep and everything stops.

## The play

People set direction and take the exceptions. An orchestrating agent runs the others and answers the routine questions, because most answers already exist in the spec, the code or the context. Two things always reach a person: design and security.

## Smells

- Someone clicking Approve all day.
- Nothing moves at night or at the weekend.
- Throughput capped by how many terminals one person can watch.

## First move

For one day, write down every question an agent asks you. Mark the ones whose answer was already written somewhere. In the case this factor comes from, that was more than nine in ten.

## In Lights Out

Each lamp is a person standing in the line. You win when all five are out.
